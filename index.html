Cultural Tourism in Australia

By Kingsley Wong

About the data

A cultural visitor takes part in at least one of the six activities explored below. Figures estimate visits or trips by people aged 15+, not unique people. Start with Australia, then zoom in to Victoria for a detailed case study.

WHAT THE DATA TELLS US

Main findings

Cultural tourism has several dimensions: the share of trips associated with culture, the places people reach, the experiences they choose and the spending across their entire stay. Australia’s largest cultural visitor markets are not necessarily those with the highest participation share. In Victoria, domestic visitors show stronger regional reach, while international visitors account for over half of cultural visitor spending.

SOURCES & READING THE DATA

Sources and credits

Tourism estimates

Creative Victoria: Victoria’s cultural visitation, based on Tourism Research Australia’s National and International Visitor Surveys, supplies cultural visits, activities, regional reach, markets and spending. Tourism figures refer to the year ending December 2024 unless another year is shown.

State share denominators combine domestic day trips, domestic overnight trips and international state visits from TRA’s December 2024 NVS results and December 2024 IVS results. The Victoria estimate reproduces the publisher’s reported share. These are related survey sources, not independent datasets.

Heritage and map boundaries

DCCEEW National Heritage List public spatial database, accessed 7 October 2026: 90 distinct place IDs with Listed place status and Historic or Indigenous classification. Source geometry is converted to approximate representative points. Licence: CC BY 3.0 AU.

State boundaries: Australian Bureau of Statistics 2021 boundaries via geoBoundaries, CC BY 4.0, simplified for display. Other Territories and remote offshore islands are omitted from these eight-state comparisons.

Reading the data

International cultural activity may occur anywhere on an Australian trip; domestic cultural activity is destination-specific. State shares combine these definitions. Survey figures are estimates.

The 2026 heritage inventory provides context alongside 2024 tourism data; it does not measure visits to heritage places. The tourism charts use the latest detailed cultural breakdown located for this project.
